Terms of use

Golf This! is a free browser game for building and playing mini golf courses. No account, purchase, or subscription is required.

Playing and creating

Course physics and scores are for entertainment. A course you create may be impossible to finish. You can edit it or choose a preset to start again.

Sharing courses

A shared link contains a copy of your course, including its name and an optional score to beat. Anyone with that link can play the course and forward it. The shared view has no course editing controls. Later edits to your saved course do not change copies you already shared. Share only text you have the right to use and avoid including personal or confidential information.

Saved progress and availability

The current course is saved in this browser when local storage is available. Clearing browser data removes it. Saving may fail in private browsing or when storage is blocked. Keep a course link if you want your own backup. Game progress is not synced between devices.
