Privacy

Golf This! runs in your browser. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, or server-side course database.

Your course stays in your browser

Your own current course, including its name and layout, is saved in localStorage under little-links-course. It stays there until replaced or removed by you or your browser. This storage lets you continue editing on a later visit. Delete it using your browser’s settings for this website. Opening a shared course does not replace your saved course. Shots and gameplay run locally. Sound is optional and starts off; enabling it only affects this session.

Course links and clipboard

Share course copies a link to your clipboard after you press the button. The course is encoded in the part of the URL after the # symbol. That fragment is not sent in the normal HTTP request to our host. Anyone you send the full link to can read its contents, and services you use to share the link may receive them. Course links are not encrypted or access-controlled. A link can also remain in your browser history. Golf This! does not read your clipboard. Challenge scores are supplied by the link creator and are not independently verified.

Loading the website

The server receives technical connection information, such as your IP address, requested page, and browser information, to deliver the website. Fonts and images are served from this website.

Contact and your rights

If you email the operator, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive information that is unnecessary for your question.
